    How We Help

    The full stack of office optimization.

    Each of these areas works on its own — or layered together for a transformation. Tell us where the friction is, and we'll map a phased rollout that pays for itself.

    Document management & distribution

    Use your MFP as a network connector. Capture, file, and retrieve hard-copy documents in a secure repository that powers the rest of your business for years.

    • Organized digital document archive
    • Faster information access
    • Streamlined paper-based processes
    • Increased employee productivity
    • Reduced carbon footprint

    Workflow & productivity

    Recently, our brand partner Xerox was named Keypoint Intelligence's 2026 Smart Workplace Solutions Line of the Year. Integrate devices into your network and transform how documents are produced & shared. Improve, redesign, or automate workflows to keep pace with changing customer demand.

    • Dynamic, integrated documents in less time
    • Easier office-wide collaboration
    • Automated manual workflows
    • Support for regulatory compliance

    Cost recovery & control

    Get visibility into the real costs of print across your business — from simple server-less tools to enterprise systems — and steer behavior with rules and quotas.

    • Prevent wasteful print behaviors
    • Set quotas or pre-defined budgets
    • Monitor usage by department or project
    • Track and bill internal cost centres
    • Detailed reporting for savings opportunities

    Security & device management

    Treat your MFP like any other endpoint. Restrict access, protect confidential output, and gain confidence that print meets the same security standards as the rest of your stack.

    • Prevent unauthorized access to print devices
    • Protect sensitive & proprietary documents
    • Promote secure collaboration
    • Support regulatory compliance efforts

    Vertical-market solutions

    We tailor configurations for Government, Health Care, Education, Legal, Manufacturing and Financial teams — with industry-specific workflows that comply out of the box.

    • Increased productivity & collaboration
    • Protection of sensitive documents
    • Automated manual workflows
    • Regulatory & sustainability compliance

    Sustainability built in

    Recently, our brand partner Canon was recognized as a Platinum Top 1% Sustainability Award Winner. Print less, scan more, and right-size your devices to actual usage. Our managed-print programs report savings — in dollars and in trees.

    • Default duplex & pull-print rules
    • Right-sized device counts & placement
    • Toner cartridge recycling program
    • Quarterly environmental reporting
