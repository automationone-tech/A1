    Black-and-White A3+ Production Press

    Walk-up simplicity meets production-level capability.

    The PrimeLink® B9125 is part of Xerox®'s PrimeLink® B9100 Series: a compact black-and-white production platform with stunning image quality, advanced automation, and configurable feeding and finishing for demanding print environments.

    For Automation One clients, it is a strong fit for in-plant print rooms and production teams that need reliable black-and-white output, flexible configuration, and local support from selection through install.

    Workflow & Usability

    Built for demanding production workflows.

    Xerox® positions the PrimeLink® B9125 around reliability, exceptional image quality, flexible finishing, workflow apps, and scalable digital front-end options including EFI and optional Fiery servers.

    Exceptional image quality

    Incredibly reliable black-and-white output designed for production environments that demand crisp text, clean solids, and consistent results job after job.

    125 ppm production speed

    Print black-and-white at up to 125 ppm to keep high-volume jobs moving while maintaining professional production output.

    Advanced finishing and feeding

    Configure binder, booklet making, cutting, slitting, folding, stacking, hole punch, and stacker options to match your job mix.

    Flexible digital front ends

    Choose workflow apps plus EFI digital front-end options, with optional Fiery EX servers for advanced job setup and production workflow control.

    Banner printing support

    Supports A3+ media and banner printing up to 13" x 26" (330 x 660 mm) for oversized applications and production flexibility.

    Sustainability credentials

    EPEAT and ENERGY STAR certified, with participation in the Xerox® Cartridge Collection Program to support responsible supplies recycling.

    Security

    Comprehensive security for production environments.

    PrimeLink® B9100 Series includes built-in security features and ConnectKey® technology to help protect device access, print data, and production workflows. Automation One can help configure the B9125 for your access rules, print queues, and finishing requirements.

    • ConnectKey platform protections designed to help safeguard device firmware and workflow data.
    • Built-in security features for device, data, and workflow protection.
    • Production-friendly workflow apps with configurable user access policies.
    • Integration with Xerox utilities for fleet and device administration.
    • Automation One support for secure deployment and production workflow configuration.
