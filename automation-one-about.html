    Our history

    Forty years on Boundary Road.

    The short version of a long story: from typewriters and fax machines through laser printers and managed-print contracts, Automation One has changed alongside the office — while staying right here in Vancouver.

    1. 1981
      Founded in Vancouver

      The Achtem family incorporates Automation One Business Systems Inc., focused on selling and servicing office equipment to local businesses.

    2. 1995
      Authorized dealer status

      Forms multi-brand partnerships and becomes an authorized dealer for Canon, Xerox, Lexmark, Francotyp Postalia, and Ideal.MBM — the same five brands we represent today.

    3. 2000
      First Consumer Choice Award

      Earns its first Vancouver Consumer Choice Award for Best Office Equipment — the start of an unbroken streak that now spans 22 consecutive years.

    4. 2008
      Burnaby office opens

      Adds the Burnaby office on Ingleton Avenue to serve clients east of the city, bringing combined parts and inventory space to over 15,000 sq. ft.

    5. 2014
      Managed Print Services launch

      Rolls out fleet management, automatic meter reads, and predictive maintenance for clients large and small — turning the service desk from reactive to proactive.

    6. 2020
      Digital solutions division

      Launches a dedicated digital-solutions practice covering document management, workflow automation, cost recovery, and device security — the foundation of the What We Do For You page.

    7. 2026
      22-year award winner

      Recognized again as Vancouver's BEST Printing Services Company by the Consumer Choice Award — the only winner in the category, locally.
